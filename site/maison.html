La Maison

A house of quiet tailoring, founded in Paris

Beni Paris was born from a simple conviction: that true elegance is effortless. Our pieces are drawn in Paris, in a palette of noir and ivoire, and made to be worn again and again — season after season.

We believe in fewer, better garments. Each collection is small and considered, built around the essentials of a wardrobe: the coat, the shirt, the trouser, and the finishing touch of cashmere.

The atelier

Cut with precision

In our atelier, every pattern is drafted by hand and every garment is fitted on the form before it is approved. Seams are pressed, buttons are sewn by hand, and each piece is inspected before it leaves the house.

We source noble materials — dense wools, fluid silks and soft cashmere — and work in small runs so that nothing is made in excess.

Client care

Caring for your pieces

Each product page lists the composition and care of the piece. Most tailoring should be dry cleaned and rested on a shaped hanger between wears; silk and cashmere prefer a gentle hand.
